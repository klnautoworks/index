Our Service Categories

We believe in honest work and clear communication—every service begins with a full inspection and a detailed explanation before any repair is done.

Brake Service

Keep your brakes safe, quiet, and responsive with our professional brake maintenance service. We inspect pads, rotors, calipers, and hardware, clean and lubricate key components, and check fluid level and condition. Perfect to prevent premature wear, noise, and vibration while keeping your stopping distance short and dependable. Ideal as part of your regular vehicle maintenance.

Learn More

Oil Change

Regular oil changes are essential to protect your engine and extend its lifespan. We replace your oil and filter, check all fluid levels, and perform a multi-point inspection of belts, hoses, and leaks. Choose from conventional, synthetic, or high-mileage oils suitable for Winnipeg’s temperature extremes.

Learn More

Diagnostics

When the check engine light comes on, our advanced diagnostic tools identify the problem quickly and accurately. We scan all vehicle systems using OBD-II technology, interpret codes, test components, and provide a detailed report with repair recommendations. Avoid guesswork and costly replacements — let our technicians find the real cause.

Learn More

Suspension & Steering

A stable and responsive suspension system ensures comfort and control. We repair and replace shocks, struts, control arms, and ball joints, correcting noise, uneven tire wear, or drifting steering. Our service includes post-repair road testing and alignment recommendations to keep your vehicle smooth and safe.

Learn More

Battery · Starter · Alternator

We test and replace your vehicle’s starting and charging system components, ensuring dependable ignition even in freezing temperatures. Our service includes load testing, voltage drop checks, and replacement with top-quality batteries and alternators. Ideal for preventing winter no-start situations common in Manitoba.

Learn More

Tires & Rotation

Regular tire rotation and inspections improve traction, fuel efficiency, and tire life. We torque your wheels to manufacturer specifications, inspect tread wear, and identify alignment issues early. Stay safe on icy, wet, or gravel roads with balanced, well-maintained tires.

Learn More

Roadside Assistance

Locked out, flat tire, or dead battery? Our 24/7 roadside assistance team responds fast anywhere in Winnipeg. We provide tire changes, jump starts, fuel delivery, and light towing for small vehicles. When you’re stranded, one call gets you back on the road — quickly and safely.

Request Help Learn More

Used Car Inspection

Buying a used vehicle? Get peace of mind with our pre-purchase inspection service. We check brakes, suspension, transmission, electrical systems, and run a full diagnostic scan. You’ll receive a digital report with photos and expert recommendations — so you know exactly what you’re buying before signing.

Learn More

Belts & Hoses

Prevent breakdowns with timely belt and hose replacements. We inspect drive belts, timing belts, and coolant hoses for cracks, leaks, or wear. Using OEM-quality parts, our technicians ensure your cooling and accessory systems run reliably for thousands of kilometers.

Learn More

Battery Replacement

Fast testing and on-site replacement to keep you starting strong in all seasons. We load-test the battery, check CCA, inspect terminals/cables, and verify alternator/charging health before we install a fresh unit.

Learn More

Fluids Flush & Replacements

Extend component life with fresh, manufacturer-spec fluids. We service brake fluid, coolant, transmission/ATF, power steering, and differentials, with proper bleed/fill procedures and leak checks.

Learn More

Fuel System Services

Restore power and smooth idle with professional fuel system cleaning. We perform injector and throttle-body cleaning, replace fuel filters (where applicable), inspect pumps/lines, and run pressure tests for accurate diagnosis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our mobile service, pricing, and the Client Portal.

How do mobile services work?

We come to your location (home or work) with professional tools. You choose a time, we perform the inspection/repair on-site, and share a digital report through our Client Portal.

What’s included in the oil change?

Each service includes synthetic oil and an oil filter replacement. Air and cabin filters are inspected and only replaced with your approval, test battery state of charge, cranking amps and overall health using a digital analizer, fluids top-off.

What can I do in the Client Portal?

Approve estimates, see photos and notes, view service history, book visits, and manage payments (when enabled)—all from your phone.

Do you offer warranty?

Yes. We warranty parts and labor according to manufacturer and service guidelines. Details are provided in your digital report and invoice.

What areas do you cover?

Winnipeg and nearby areas. For jobs outside city limits, a travel fee may apply—ask us when you book.
