Some providers may process data outside Canada (e.g., the United States). We take steps to protect your information consistent with this Policy and applicable laws.

7. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law (e.g., tax/audit obligations).

8. Your choices and rights

11. Updates
