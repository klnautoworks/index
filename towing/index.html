Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you get to me?

Dispatch runs 24/7. We give you an estimated arrival time when we confirm your request. Actual times depend on weather, traffic and call volume.

How much does a tow cost?

It depends on the distance, the type of job and any special equipment needed, such as dollies or winching. We’ll tell you how your price is worked out before the job starts. Applicable taxes apply.

Can you tow outside Winnipeg?

Yes. We cover Winnipeg and surrounding areas and take out-of-town requests too—call us with the destination.

Where can you take my vehicle?

To the shop of your choice, your home, or another destination you give us.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your exact location, your vehicle (year, make and model), where you want it taken, and whether it can roll and steer, has AWD/4x4, or has keys available. Don’t worry if you’re unsure—dispatch will walk you through it.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, credit, debit and e-Transfer.

Do you also repair vehicles?

Yes. KLN’s mobile mechanics handle brakes, diagnostics, batteries, suspension and more at your location. See mechanic services.
