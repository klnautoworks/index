Our Story & Mission

Building trust and convenience through mobile auto care.

Putting Drivers First

KLN AutoWorks was founded by lifelong automotive technicians who were frustrated by the long wait times, hidden fees and impersonal service of traditional garages. After seeing that 60% of drivers prefer mobile mechanics over brick‑and‑mortar shops, we set out to bring the shop to you.

What sets us apart? A relentless focus on honesty, communication and quality. You’ll meet your mechanic, get a clear price before the work starts and receive a digital report afterwards. We treat customers like neighbours—not numbers.
