Oil & Maintenance Services

Routine maintenance done right—without leaving your driveway.

Keep Your Engine Running Smoothly

Regular oil changes and maintenance are essential for engine health. Our mobile service lets you stay productive while we handle the messy work. InstaMek notes that on‑site oil changes eliminate trips to the shop and ensure vehicles stay in top condition.

What We Offer

  • Conventional, synthetic and high‑mileage oil changes
  • Filter replacements (oil, air, fuel and cabin)
  • Fluid top‑ups (coolant, brake, power steering, washer)
  • Tire rotation and pressure checks
  • Multi‑point inspections to spot issues early

Signs You Need an Oil Change

  • Check engine or oil change light illuminated
  • Engine noise, knocking or rough idling
  • Dark, dirty or gritty oil on the dipstick
  • Oil smells burnt or there is smoke from the exhaust
  • Decreased fuel economy or sluggish acceleration

  • Protect your factory warranty with OEM‑approved parts and methods
  • Clear upfront pricing and digital service reports
