Fluids Flush & Replacement

Protect critical systems with OEM-spec fluids and proper service at your doorstep.

Fresh Fluids. Longer Life. Better Performance.

Fluids carry heat, pressure, and lubrication through your vehicle. When they degrade, parts wear faster and safety systems can fail. We replace and bleed fluids using the correct specifications, procedures, and torque values—saving you time and preventing costly repairs down the road.

What We Offer

  • Brake Fluid Exchange (DOT 3/4/5.1) with proper ABS bleed procedures
  • Coolant/Antifreeze Service — drain & fill or full exchange, vacuum fill to remove air
  • Transmission Fluid Service (ATF/CVT) — pan & filter where applicable; no-risk procedures aligned with OEM guidance
  • Differential & Transfer Case fluid replacement with new crush washers
  • Power Steering Fluid exchange (where serviceable)
  • Leak inspection, fluid condition report, and service light/maintenance reminder reset (if supported)
  • OEM-spec fluids that meet the required approvals for your make & model

When to Schedule

  • Spongy brake pedal, dark or contaminated brake fluid
  • Overheating, coolant discoloration, or sweet smell under hood
  • Harsh/rough shifting, delayed engagement, or fluid burnt smell
  • Whining power steering or heavy steering effort
  • At manufacturer intervals or every 2–3 years for brake/coolant (model-dependent)

Why Choose KLN?

  • Mobile convenience—no shop trips or waiting rooms.
  • Correct specs, procedures, and torque values—done right the first time.
  • Transparent quotes with fluid types and quantities listed.
  • Environmentally responsible handling and disposal of old fluids.
  • 12-month/20,000 km workmanship warranty.
